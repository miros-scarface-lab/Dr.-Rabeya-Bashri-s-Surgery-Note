Clinical Procedures & Care

Surgical & Obstetric Services

From gentle childbirth to advanced laparoscopic operative gynecology, delivering world-class surgical precision and empathetic care.

Obstetric & Delivery Care

Safe maternal management for mother and newborn

Normal & Painless Vaginal Delivery

Natural labor support in a calm, mother-centric environment. We offer painless epidural analgesia administered by specialized anesthesiologists, enabling smooth, comfortable labor with continuous CTG fetal heart surveillance.

Clinical Highlights:
  • Epidural analgesia for painless labor
  • Trial of Labor After Cesarean (VBAC) evaluation
  • Gentle perineal preservation techniques

Cesarean Delivery (LSCS)

Elective and emergency Lower Segment Cesarean Sections (LSCS) performed in tertiary hospital theatres with full neonatal resuscitation and NICU backup. Cosmetically closed with hidden subcuticular sutures.

Indications & Care:
  • Previous repeat C-sections (1, 2, or 3 scars)
  • Twin pregnancies, breech and transverse lie
  • Placenta previa & maternal preeclampsia management

Cervical Cerclage & High-Risk Obstetrics

Prophylactic and rescue cervical encirclement (McDonald stitch) to prevent second-trimester miscarriages and preterm labor in women with cervical incompetence.

Surgical Highlights:
  • Early 12-14 week elective stitch placement
  • Emergency ectopic pregnancy salpingectomy
  • Post-cerclage preterm delivery prevention protocols

Gynecological & Laparoscopic Surgeries

Open and minimally invasive keyhole operations

Hysterectomy (TAH / VH / TLH)

Definitive surgical treatment for severe menorrhagia, massive adenomyosis, uterine fibroids, and uterine prolapse. Approaches tailored to patient anatomy and preferences.

  • Total Abdominal Hysterectomy (TAH)
  • Vaginal Hysterectomy with Pelvic Floor Repair
  • Laparoscopic Hysterectomy (LAVH/TLH)

Uterus-Preserving Myomectomy

Specialized enucleation and excision of uterine fibroids while meticulously preserving uterine anatomy and fertility for women planning future childbearing.

  • Multiple intramural & subserosal fibroids
  • Tourniquet & vasopressin blood-sparing protocols
  • Multi-layer uterine reconstruction

Laparoscopic Ovarian Cystectomy

Minimally invasive keyhole surgery to excise chocolate cysts (endometriosis), dermoid cysts, or persistent ovarian neoplasms while preserving healthy ovarian tissue.

  • Preservation of ovarian reserve (AMH preservation)
  • Endometriosis ablation & adhesiolysis
  • Diagnostic chromopertubation for infertility
Patient Guidance

What to Expect During Surgery

Clear information to prepare you mentally and physically for a smooth surgical experience.

📋 Before Your Surgery (Pre-Operative)

  • Anesthesia Assessment: Meet with our senior anesthesiologist to review blood tests, ECG, and choose between spinal or general anesthesia.
  • Fasting Guidelines: Strictly avoid food and drinks for 6 to 8 hours prior to the scheduled surgical hour.
  • Medications: Specific instructions on which routine medicines (e.g., thyroid, hypertension) to take with a small sip of water.
  • Hospital Admission: Arrive at the OT reception at least 2 hours before the scheduled time with prior ultrasound reports.

🌸 After Your Surgery (Recovery & Discharge)

  • Pain Management: Modern multi-modal analgesic protocols ensuring maximum comfort and early mobilization.
  • Early Ambulation: Encouraged to sit up and walk gently within 12 to 24 hours to accelerate intestinal motility and avoid thrombosis.
  • Discharge Timeline: Routine C-sections are discharged on Day 2 or 3; laparoscopic procedures often discharged within 24 to 48 hours.
  • Follow-Up: A scheduled stitch check / wound inspection visit at 7 to 10 days with Dr. Rabeya.
