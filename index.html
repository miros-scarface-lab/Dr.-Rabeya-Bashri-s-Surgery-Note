Dr. Rabeya Basri is a dedicated Trainee in Obstetrics & Gynecology with over 5 years of active clinical experience in maternal-fetal medicine and operative gynecology. Currently pursuing her FCPS in Obstetrics & Gynecology from the Bangladesh College of Physicians and Surgeons (BCPS), she has managed over 1,700 surgical deliveries and procedures, providing compassionate, evidence-based care to more than 15,000 patients.

Dr. Rabeya has performed over 1,700 successful surgical deliveries and procedures, including routine and high-risk Cesarean sections, complex repeat C-sections, and multi-fetal pregnancies. She is a dedicated advocate for natural, gentle, and painless deliveries with advanced continuous fetal monitoring.

In gynecological surgery, she is recognized for her expertise in uterine preservation techniques (abdominal and laparoscopic myomectomy for fibroids), laparoscopic ovarian cystectomies, and minimally invasive hysterectomies. Her surgical philosophy is rooted in meticulous technique, minimal blood loss, cosmetic incision closure, and rapid postoperative recovery.
