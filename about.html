Dedicated to Safe Deliveries & Advanced Gynecological Care

Dr. Rabeya Basri is an experienced Consultant Obstetrician and Gynecologist with over 15 years of surgical excellence in maternal-fetal medicine and operative gynecology. Having completed her FCPS from the Bangladesh College of Physicians and Surgeons (BCPS) and Master of Surgery (MS), she has dedicated her career to providing compassionate, evidence-based care to women across all stages of life.

Dr. Rabeya has performed thousands of successful surgical deliveries, including routine and high-risk Cesarean sections, complex repeat C-sections (previous 2 and 3 scars), and multi-fetal pregnancies. She is a strong advocate for natural and painless delivery, ensuring mothers receive the highest standard of labor support, epidural analgesia, and fetal monitoring.

In gynecological surgery, she is recognized for her expertise in uterine preservation techniques (abdominal and laparoscopic myomectomy for fibroids), laparoscopic ovarian cystectomies, and minimally invasive hysterectomies. Her surgical philosophy is rooted in meticulous technique, minimal blood loss, cosmetic incision closure, and rapid postoperative recovery.

🌸 Gentle Birth Advocacy
Encouraging natural and painless labor whenever clinically safe for mother and baby.
🔬 Precision Surgery
Minimally invasive keyhole techniques for faster recovery and reduced pain.
